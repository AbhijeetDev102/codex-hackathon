# ADR-002: Evidence-first claims and outcome assessment

**Status:** accepted

## Decision

Every factual claim must cite valid evidence. The system checks quotes, dates/numbers, and whether the evidence supports the claim. Unsupported claims are removed. Outcome assessment is a deterministic, evidence-linked graph node with explicit statuses; distress alone is not failure.

## Why

Public data can show likely contributors but not prove one private decision caused a business failure.
